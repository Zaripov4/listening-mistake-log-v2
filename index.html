      How to use

      1. Do one Listening practice test, like the real exam: listen only once, no pausing. Use real tests (Cambridge IELTS books are best).
      2. Check your answers. Keep the transcript (the text of the recording) open. It's at the back of the book.
      3. For each wrong answer, tap Check a wrong answer. Pick the kind of question, type your answer and the right answer. The app may ask you 1–3 easy yes/no questions.
      4. Type your score out of 40 under My scores.
      5. Look at Your top 3 mistakes. Before the next test, practise only the fix for #1, and play its game.

      XP: +10 for each wrong answer you check, +10 for each score, +5 for each right answer to a new game question (+1 if you have seen it before). Use the app on different days to grow your 🔥 streak.

      Tip: add this page to your home screen so it opens like an app. iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
